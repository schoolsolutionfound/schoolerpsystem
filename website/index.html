What it covers

Six parts of a campus that usually live in six different places. In Kivquo they share the same student record.

  1. 01 Admissions Enquiries, applications, document checks and seat confirmation in one flow. Admissions desk
  2. 02 Attendance and academics Daily attendance, timetables, exams and report cards, kept in step with each class. Faculty
  3. 03 Fees and payroll Fee schedules, receipts, dues and staff salaries without parallel registers. Accounts
  4. 04 Transport Routes, stops, buses and live tracking for the families who ask. Transport
  5. 05 Library Issue, return, fines and catalogue, tied to the student record. Library
  6. 06 Communication Notices, announcements and transactional email that reach the right group. Everyone

Built for the way a campus actually works

Every role sees the same facts. The office stops reconciling lists, teachers stop chasing registers, and families get a straight answer the first time.

Getting started

A rollout is mostly a migration, not a rewrite. Most of the work happens before the first login.

  1. 01

    Import

    Students, staff, classes, fee heads and transport routes, brought over from your existing registers.

  2. 02

    Configure

    Roles, permissions, timetables and the way your institution names things.

  3. 03

    Run

    Staff sign in, families get updates, and reports come from live data rather than month-end files.
