# Student

## Purpose

A Student is a learner enrolled in an institution.

Students are the primary beneficiaries of the ERP and access academic information generated throughout their educational journey.

Students cannot manage institutional data and only have access to information that belongs to them.

---

# Student Lifecycle

Every student follows the same lifecycle.

Institution Administrator
    ↓
Student Created
    ↓
Student Assigned To Institution
    ↓
Student Assigned Academic Structure
    ↓
Firebase Account Created
    ↓
Student First Login
    ↓
Change Temporary Password
    ↓
Complete Profile
    ↓
Student Active

---

# Student Information

Every student record contains academic and personal information.

Examples include:

Academic Information

- Roll Number / USN
- Admission Number (Optional)
- Institution
- Department
- Academic Year
- Course (If Applicable)

Personal Information

- First Name
- Last Name
- Email
- Phone Number
- Date of Birth
- Gender
- Address

Additional information may be introduced in future versions.

---

# Academic Association

Every student belongs to exactly one institution.

A student may also belong to:

- Department
- Academic Year
- Course
- Section (if applicable)

These relationships determine the student's academic environment.

---

# Student Account

Every student has a user account.

The account is created by the system.

Students cannot register themselves.

The account is used for:

- Authentication
- Profile Management
- Accessing Academic Information

---

# Student Responsibilities

Students can:

- View attendance
- View marks
- View assignments
- View timetable
- View announcements
- View notifications
- Update personal profile information

Students cannot modify academic records.

---

# Student Profile

During the first login, students complete their profile.

Profile completion may include:

- Profile Photo
- Contact Information
- Emergency Contact
- Personal Details

Institutions may require additional information based on their policies.

---

# Student Status

A student may have one of the following statuses:

- Active
- Inactive
- Graduated
- Suspended
- Alumni (Future)

Student status determines access to the platform and academic operations.

---

# Student Data Ownership

Every student owns only their personal information.

Academic records such as:

- Attendance
- Marks
- Assignments
- Results

are created by authorized institutional users and cannot be modified by students.

---

# Student Visibility

Students can only access information that belongs to them.

Students cannot:

- View other students' data
- View teacher information
- Access administrative features
- Access another institution's data

---

# Guiding Principles

Students are consumers of academic information.

They do not participate in institutional administration.

Every student belongs to exactly one institution, and all academic activities are performed within that institution's context.
