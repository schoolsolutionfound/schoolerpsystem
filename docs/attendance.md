# Attendance

## Purpose

Attendance records the daily presence of students during scheduled academic sessions.

Attendance is created by teachers and becomes part of the student's permanent academic record.

Attendance is institution-specific and cannot be shared across institutions.

---

# Responsibility

Attendance is managed by teachers.

Institution Administrators oversee attendance but do not perform daily attendance marking.

Students can only view their own attendance.

---

# Attendance Flow

Institution Administrator
    ↓
Creates Teachers
    ↓
Creates Students
    ↓
Assigns Teachers
    ↓
Teacher Logs In
    ↓
Selects Class
    ↓
Selects Subject
    ↓
Marks Attendance
    ↓
Attendance Saved
    ↓
Student Can View Attendance

---

# Attendance Session

Attendance is recorded during an attendance session.

Each attendance session belongs to:

- Institution
- Department
- Academic Year
- Class / Section
- Subject
- Teacher
- Date

Each session contains the attendance records for all students in that class.

---

# Attendance Status

A student may have one of the following attendance statuses:

- Present
- Absent
- Late
- Excused Leave

Additional statuses may be introduced in future versions.

---

# Attendance Record

Each attendance record belongs to exactly one:

- Attendance Session
- Student

Each record stores:

- Student
- Status
- Timestamp
- Recorded By

---

# Attendance Rules

Attendance can only be created by the assigned teacher.

Teachers cannot record attendance for classes that are not assigned to them.

Students cannot modify attendance.

Institution Administrators may review attendance but do not modify daily attendance records.

---

# Editing Attendance

Attendance correction policies are configurable and may vary between institutions.

After the correction window expires, attendance becomes locked.

Institution Administrators may unlock attendance if institutional policy allows.

The exact correction policy may vary between institutions.

---

# Viewing Attendance

Teachers can view attendance for their assigned classes.

Institution Administrators can view attendance for their institution.

Students can only view their own attendance history.

Users cannot access attendance belonging to another institution.

---

# Attendance Reports

Attendance data is used to generate reports such as:

- Daily Attendance
- Monthly Attendance
- Subject-wise Attendance
- Student Attendance Summary
- Class Attendance Summary

Additional analytics may be introduced in future versions.

---

# Notifications

Attendance events may generate notifications.

Examples include:

- Student marked absent
- Low attendance warning
- Monthly attendance summary

Notification rules depend on institution configuration.

---

# Guiding Principles

Attendance is created once and becomes part of the student's academic history.

Every attendance record belongs to an institution.

Attendance must be accurate, traceable, and auditable.

Only authorized users may create or modify attendance records.
